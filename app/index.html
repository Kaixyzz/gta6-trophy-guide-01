    Roadmap to the platinum

    PowerPyx rates this platinum 4 out of 10 in difficulty and about 200 hours, with a single playthrough enough. The time goes on the 100% checklist, the stunts and the WAiNK spots — not on hard fights. Work the phases in order.

    1. Phase 1 — Play the story, and mind the one missable

      Chapters 1 to 4 plus the final mission, about 60 to 80 hours. Meditate at the Chapter 1 spot the moment it is offered — it is the only trophy that can be missed, and there is no mission replay. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter. Pick up stranger missions as they appear, and start scanning vehicles and studying animals with the binoculars from the start.

      Trophies: all story trophies, Positive Mental Attitude, the stranger strands, Master of None.

    2. Phase 2 — Robberies and partner jobs

      After the story opens the map up, work the robbery trophies: bank vaults, armored trucks, jewelry stores, shops with your partner, and one illegal operation per gang. Plan each getaway before you go in, and keep a fast car waiting. Launder marked bills through your contacts as you go so Dime on the Dollar builds up on its own.

      Trophies: Maximum Withdrawal, A Classic Blitz Play, Sneakthief, Retail Therapy, Hot Together, Pest Control, Dime on the Dollar.

    3. Phase 3 — Collectibles and the long grinds

      The slow burn: 250 stunts, WAiNK at 100 locations, three card sets, 100 vehicles discovered, all of Leonida explored, every garage bought, ten vehicles registered. Do a little of each every session instead of saving one grind for the end.

      Trophies: Cunning Stunts, You WAiNKer!, Don’t Touch My Deck, Whip It Good, The Goodtime State, Little Boxes, On the Spot.

    4. Phase 4 — Cleanup and the 100% checklist

      Open Progress, then Stats, then 100% Completion and work the checklist. You need any 70 of the 80 challenges and 50 for One Last Thing, so skip the ones you hate. The shooting range golds, drone race wins and the remaining activities finish here. A Long Road pops when the checklist is done, and the platinum follows.

      Trophies: A Long Road, One Last Thing, The Shootist, Top Fun, Earn Cash, Not Kudos, and whatever is left.

    Two habits for the whole run: keep cheats off — they disable trophies and saving — and remember there are no online trophies, so everything on this list is yours alone to earn.

    Missable trophies

    Good news first: almost nothing on this list can be missed. The published guides agree there is only one trophy to worry about.

    Three habits that keep you safe

    • Save at every chapter. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so a manual save at the start of each chapter is your only way back.
    • Do the Chapter 1 meditation at once. Do not walk past it to do something else first.
    • Keep cheats off. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so a cheat-filled session can cost you more than it saves.

    Everything else on the list stays available in free-roam after the story, so the post-story cleanup in Phase 4 can catch anything you skipped.
