Platinum roadmap

One playthrough is enough, no online trophies, and cheats disable trophies — keep them off until you are done. PowerPyx rates the platinum about 200 hours.

  1. Play the story (60–80 hours). Chapter endings and seven story missions award trophies on their own. Do the Positive Mental Attitude meditation with Lucia in Chapter 1 when it is offered — it may be the only missable one.
  2. Finish the Strangers & Freaks strands. Eight strands earn the silver Strangers & Freaks, and Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone each have their own final-mission trophy.
  3. Clear the activities. Gold in all 42 Shooting Range challenges, drone races at all 14 locations, 20 different races, an arcade game, Cal’s fishing challenge and every meditation spot.
  4. Do the crime trophies with your partner. Five bank vaults, an armored truck, a jewelry store without being seen, three shops in a row and an illegal operation from every gang.
  5. Collect along the way. Three card sets, 100 WAiNK scans, 100 discovered vehicles, every garage, $100,000 spent in shops and $250,000 laundered.
  6. Finish 100% completion for A Long Road (gold). That pops the platinum, Vice Got a Price!.
