Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough is enough — nothing here needs online play, and after the story the whole map stays open for clean-up. Work in this order:

  1. Play the story at your leisure. Twelve story trophies pop on their own, plus the gold for the final mission. Enjoy Leonida first; the checklist can wait.
  2. Meditate everywhere, early. Positive Mental Attitude is the one trophy that can be missed — one of its 18 meditation spots becomes unavailable later. Meditate at every spot the moment you find it, and wait for the final zen animation.
  3. Try everything once. Sample all 9 activity types for Master of None, start the Stranger strands as they appear, and scan with WAiNK whenever you pass a landmark.
  4. Finish the story, then open the 100% checklist (Touch Pad > Progress > 100% Completion). A Long Road is the hardest trophy and drives the whole endgame — most other trophies fall out while you work through it.
  5. Clean up the grinds last: 250 stunts, 42 Shooting Range gold medals, 50 of the 80 Challenges, and the remaining collectible card sets.

Guides estimate the platinum at roughly 200–300 hours. Ten trophies carry methods the community is still confirming; their guides are labelled Strategy from the description and say so honestly.
